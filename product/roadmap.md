## 2. Phase Breakdown

### Phase 1: Core Web MVP (Web Platform Only)
- **Objective:** Deliver a complete, high-performance end-to-end **Web Application** baseline for knowledge management, task execution, daily journaling, and system navigation.
- **Included Modules:** Modules 1 (Web Dashboard & Command Center), 2 (Universal Web Capture), 4 (Second Brain Notes), 6 (Tasks), 7 (Projects), 8 (Goals), 10 (Web Calendar), 16 (Daily Journal), 17 (Full-Text & Command Search), 18 (Life Graph Link Registry), 27 (Auth, SSO, MFA, Tenant Isolation).
- **Target Platforms:** **Web Application Only** (Optimized for Web Browsers on Desktop, Laptop, and Mobile Viewports).

### Phase 2: System Expansion & Multi-Platform Clients (Android & macOS)
- **Objective:** Extend Life OS into native desktop and mobile platforms with offline-first synchronization, native system trays, mobile share intents, and team collaboration.
- **Included Modules:** Modules 3 (Chat), 5 (Whiteboard/Draw.io), 9 (Areas), 11 (Habits), 12 (Metrics), 13 (Finance), 14 (Documents/OCR), 15 (Reminders), 24 (Local-First Offline Sync), 25 (Universal Archival), 26 (Auto Backup & Restore).
- **Target Platforms:** **macOS / Windows Desktop Native App** (Tauri + System Tray + Global Hotkeys), **Android Mobile Native App** (React Native + Push Notifications + Share Sheet), **Web PWA**.

### Phase 3: Intelligence & Automation Layer
- **Objective:** Integrate context-aware AI, autonomous multi-agent swarms, semantic RAG search, and automated workflows.
- **Included Modules:** Modules 17 (Semantic RAG), 19 (AI Assistant), 20 (Multi-Agent System), 21 (Rule Automation), 22 (Reviews & Analytics), 23 (Custom Fields & Schemas).
